About

I'm a research scientist and PhD student at Johannes Kepler University Linz, based in Vienna. My work sits where machine learning meets music and robotics.

I studied Technical Physics at TU Wien before switching to an MSc in Artificial Intelligence at JKU. My master's thesis, Difficulty Estimation and Robotic Piano Performance via Reinforcement Learning, put a humanoid robot in front of a piano. Today I mostly work on real-time score following: getting a computer to know, at every moment, where a live musician is in the score.

I've also taught Deep Learning & Neural Networks as a teaching assistant at JKU, tutored mathematics for several years, and coordinate the Plant-Based Universities campaign at JKU Linz.

Research & Code

Most of what I build is open source, and most of it orbits one question: how does a machine keep up with a human performer?

Track A — Real-time score following

Library · contributor PyPI

matchmaker

A Python library for real-time music alignment. Offline alignment has solid public implementations, but online score followers used to be rebuilt from scratch for every project. Matchmaker provides efficient reference implementations for audio and MIDI input, ready to drop into apps such as automatic accompaniment or page turning. 

$ pip install pymatchmaker
  • Python
  • Audio + MIDI
  • DTW · HMM
pymatchmaker/matchmaker →
Benchmark · contributor

matchmaker_benchmark

An open leaderboard for score following. You write one Python file and open a pull request; CI then evaluates your follower on 146 fixed performances (ASAP, Batik, Vienna 4x22) and ranks it by beat error, ms error, tracking rate and real-time factor. Nobody has to download any data.

  • Leaderboard
  • GitHub Actions
pymatchmaker/matchmaker_benchmark →
Method · author public soon

rl_follower

Score followers learned with reinforcement learning (trained with DAgger against an exact oracle). One model per modality follows any piece without retraining: an event-based follower for MIDI and a frame-based follower for audio that corrects a tempo-aware online DTW with a learned residual policy. The audio follower reaches a 0.938 tracking rate on the benchmark's eval fold.

  • RL · DAgger
  • Matchmaker plugin
Game · Gym env · author public soon

FollowTheScore

A score-following game and Gymnasium environment. While a piano performance plays, a little character hops from note onset to note onset, and the goal is to land where the pianist actually is. You can play it yourself, race a matchmaker follower, watch an RL agent, or use it as a coding challenge with a leaderboard.

  • pygame
  • Gymnasium
  • Teaching
Bibliography · contributor

score-following-bibliography

A curated, verified bibliography of real-time score following research, maintained alongside matchmaker and modelled on the OMR Research bibliography. The BibTeX files come with keyword filters and a generated static site. New papers are found through Zotero and OpenAlex citations.

Thesis project · author code public soon

Full-body RoboPianist

This extends RoboPianist from two floating hands to a full-body humanoid. A Unitree G1 with Shadow Dexterous Hands learns to play piano pieces through deep reinforcement learning, from Twinkle Twinkle to Bach, Chopin and MGMT. I built the robot model as the open-source Humanoid Shadow G1 (MuJoCo Menagerie style).

Plant-Based Universities

“Practice what you teach.” Universities produce much of the research on food and climate. Their canteens should reflect it.

Plant-Based Universities is a student-led campaign that started at three UK universities in 2021. It asks universities to move to 100% plant-based catering as a response to the climate and biodiversity crises. It has since spread across Europe. In Austria, the University of Graz moved to a fully plant-based canteen, and the University of Vienna committed to 70% plant-based catering.

I coordinate the campaign at JKU Linz. We work with students, the student union and campus catering to make plant-based food the default on campus. If you study or work at JKU and want to get involved, get in touch.
