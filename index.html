Research & Code

Music is something people make together. I study what happens when one of the partners is a computer: one that listens closely, follows the musician's tempo, and helps with accompaniment, page turning or practice.

Track A — Real-time score following

Library · contributor PyPI

matchmaker

A Python library for real-time music alignment, with reference score followers for audio and MIDI that drop straight into applications like automatic accompaniment or page turning. 

$ pip install pymatchmaker
  • Python
  • Audio + MIDI
  • DTW · HMM

System · contributor

ACCompanion

An expressive accompaniment system for piano that follows the soloist in real time. For its 0.4.0 release I brought in matchmaker's score followers, waiting at fermatas and free-tempo passages, and MIDI recording.

  • Python
  • Real-time
  • Accompaniment
CPJKU/accompanion →

matchmaker_benchmark

An open leaderboard for score following. Submit one Python file and CI evaluates your follower on 146 fixed performances.

Plant-Based Universities

“Practice what you teach.” Universities should serve what their climate research recommends.

Plant-Based Universities is a student-led campaign calling for 100% plant-based catering at universities. It started in the UK in 2021 and has since reached campuses across Europe, including Graz and Vienna.

I coordinate the campaign at JKU Linz. If you study or work at JKU, sign up or get involved on our campaign page.
