About

Research scientist and PhD student at JKU Linz, based in Vienna, working where machine learning meets music and robotics. I'm happiest when research turns into something people can actually use.

I hold a BSc in Technical Physics from TU Wien and an MSc in Artificial Intelligence from JKU, where my master's thesis put a humanoid robot at the piano. Today I mostly work on real-time score following.

As a student I was a teaching assistant for JKU's Deep Learning courses and tutored mathematics. Today I also coordinate Plant-Based Universities at JKU Linz.

02

Research & Code

Music is something people make together. I study what happens when one of the partners is a computer: one that listens closely, follows the musician's tempo, and helps with accompaniment, page turning or practice.

Track A — Real-time score following

Library · contributor PyPI

matchmaker

A Python library for real-time music alignment, with reference score followers for audio and MIDI that drop straight into applications like automatic accompaniment or page turning. 

$ pip install pymatchmaker
  • Python
  • Audio + MIDI
  • DTW · HMM
pymatchmaker/matchmaker →
Benchmark · contributor

matchmaker_benchmark

An open leaderboard for score following. Submit one Python file and CI evaluates your follower on 146 fixed performances.

  • Leaderboard
  • GitHub Actions
pymatchmaker/matchmaker_benchmark →
Method · author public soon

rl_follower

Score followers learned with reinforcement learning: one model per modality (MIDI or audio) follows any piece without retraining, reaching a 0.938 tracking rate on audio in the benchmark.

  • RL · DAgger
  • Matchmaker plugin
Game · Gym env · author public soon

FollowTheScore

A score-following game and Gymnasium environment: hop through the score along with a piano performance, race a computer follower, or train an RL agent.

  • pygame
  • Gymnasium
  • Teaching

Plant-Based Universities

“Practice what you teach.” Universities should serve what their climate research recommends.

Plant-Based Universities is a student-led campaign calling for 100% plant-based catering at universities. It started in the UK in 2021 and has since reached campuses across Europe, including Graz and Vienna.

I coordinate the campaign at JKU Linz. If you study or work at JKU, sign up or get involved on our campaign page.
